01 / Analysis & validationPHISEC Racing

CHASSIS STRUCTURAL ANALYSIS

Evaluating torsional stiffness and chassis safety through finite element analysis of torsional, frontal and side-impact loading cases.

SolidWorks SimulationFEAStructural mechanics

The challenge. Understand how the chassis responds to different load cases and how modelling assumptions affect the result.

My contribution. Worked on chassis FEA, comparing beam, shell and solid modelling approaches and considering their influence on the structural assessment.

Engineering lesson. Boundary conditions and idealisation matter as much as the solver: results need to be interpreted against the physical structure.

ENGINEERING THE BIGGER PICTURE

Mechanical direction for a Formula Student project, connecting design decisions, structural requirements and manufacturing considerations.

Mechanical designTeam leadershipDesign for manufacture

The role. Mechanical Director at PHISEC Racing since September 2025, following a first year in the chassis department.

Design in practice. During hand calculations for the ASES, a structural segment exceeded yield stress under the prescribed load. We redesigned it to address the requirement.

The perspective. Performance depends on the complete system: individual components must work together and be practical to manufacture.
